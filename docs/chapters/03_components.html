System Components and Architectures#

PEM Cell and Stack Design#

Basic structure includes anode, cathode, membrane, and flow fields. Stack designs vary by manufacturer and performance targets.

Cell Components#

Catalyst Layers and Membranes#

Catalyst selection affects efficiency and cost. Membranes must ensure high proton conductivity and chemical stability.

Balance of Plant (BoP)#

Includes pumps, compressors, humidifiers, and control electronics essential for operation and safety.

Aging and Degradation Mechanisms#

Key failure modes include catalyst dissolution, membrane thinning, and contamination.

Characterization Methods#

Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS)#

Used to analyze charge transfer and diffusion processes in situ.

Cyclic Voltammetry (CV)#

Useful for evaluating catalyst activity and electrochemical surface area.

Cross-sectional SEM and XPS#

Structural and chemical analysis of aged membranes and catalyst layers.

Polarization Curve and Durability Testing#

Standard metrics for assessing cell performance and lifespan.
